Diagram

Working range ends here.
The last 15–20° is where elbows get hurt.

Equipment
REP preacher pad · Rogue bench · one REP dumbbell (start 20–30 lb)
Set up
Pad on the bench, angled face toward you. One armpit over the top edge, that upper arm flat on the pad, chest against it. Free hand braces the pad.
Execute
Curl the dumbbell up, squeeze 2 seconds, lower over 3. Let the forearm rotate naturally through the rep instead of being locked by a bar. Right arm first, then match it with the left.
Elbow

STOP 15–20° SHORT OF FULL EXTENSION at the bottom. Never let the arm straighten completely under load with the upper arm pinned to a pad — that position is one of the most common ways lifters wreck an elbow. One arm at a time also frees the wrist: a fixed bar locks your forearm into one rotation for every single rep, and that lock is a common driver of elbow pain.
